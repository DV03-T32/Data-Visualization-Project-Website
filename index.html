Australian speeding enforcement, 2008-2024

A dashboard exploring speeding infringements detected by cameras and police across Australian jurisdictions.

About this dashboard

The T32 Speeding Dashboard summarises speeding fines issued between 2008 and 2024, highlighting the shift from police-issued fines to technology-based enforcement such as fixed and mobile cameras.

It is designed for policymakers, enforcement agencies, journalists and the public to compare trends across jurisdictions and detection methods using a high data-ink ratio and minimal visual noise.
