Australian speeding enforcement, 2008-2024

A dashboard exploring speeding infringements detected by cameras and police across Australian jurisdictions.

About this dashboard

The T32 Speeding Dashboard summarises speeding fines issued between 2008 and 2024, highlighting the shift from police-issued fines to technology-based enforcement such as fixed and mobile cameras.

It is designed for policymakers, enforcement agencies, journalists and the public to compare trends across jurisdictions and detection methods using a high data-ink ratio and minimal visual noise.

Data Collection Process

  • Police Enforcement datasets are compiled by BITRE using data submitted by state and territory policing and transport agencies.
  • Agencies contributing data include:
    • NSW Police Force
    • Victoria Police
    • Queensland Police Service
    • Department of Transport and Main Roads (QLD)
    • South Australia Police
    • Western Australia Police Force
    • Tasmania Police & Department of Police and Emergency Management
    • Northern Territory Police
    • Australian Federal Police
    • Access Canberra
    • Australian Infrastructure and Transport Statistics Yearbook
  • Each jurisdiction reports annual data and, from 2023 onwards, monthly enforcement activity.
  • BITRE aggregates, validates, and standardises all submissions before publishing on the Road Safety Data Hub.
